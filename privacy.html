Introduction

Batuhan Das ("Developer", "we", "us", "our") operates the WiseCal mobile application ("App", "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App. Please read this policy carefully. By using WiseCal, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect different types of information to provide and improve the App:

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Screenshots/Images: When you share a screenshot or image with the App, text is extracted from the image on your device using Apple Vision framework. Only the extracted text is sent to our server for AI processing. The original image is never uploaded to any server and is discarded immediately after on-device text extraction.
  • Calendar Data: When you confirm an event, the App creates entries in your device calendar via Apple EventKit. We do not read, store, or transmit your existing calendar data.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Analytics Data: We collect anonymous usage data such as feature usage counts, error types, and app performance metrics. This data contains no personally identifiable information (PII). Analytics data is collected via Firebase Analytics.
  • Device Identifier: A non-personal device identifier is used solely for analytics purposes. This identifier is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking across apps or websites.
  • Subscription Status: We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat may collect an anonymous app user ID and purchase-related data. No personal information is shared with RevenueCat.

1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location data, contacts, browsing history, or any other personally identifiable information. We do not require account creation to use the App.

3. How We Process Your Images

Understanding how your images are processed is important to us:

  • Step 1 — On-Device OCR: Your image is processed entirely on your device using Apple Vision framework. No image data leaves your device.
  • Step 2 — Text Transmission: Only the extracted text (not the image) is sent to our secure Firebase Functions server.
  • Step 3 — AI Processing: The text is processed by Google Gemini AI to identify appointment details (date, time, location, description).
  • Step 4 — Immediate Deletion: The extracted text is not stored on our servers. It is processed in real-time and discarded immediately after the response is generated.

5. Data Storage and Retention

  • Images/Screenshots: Never uploaded. Discarded immediately after on-device OCR processing.
  • Extracted Text: Processed in real-time by AI. Not stored on our servers.
  • Calendar Events: Stored locally on your device, managed by Apple's calendar system.
  • Analytics Data: Anonymous data retained for up to 90 days, then automatically deleted.
  • Subscription Data: Managed by RevenueCat and Apple. We do not store payment information.
  • App Preferences: Stored locally on your device using UserDefaults and Keychain.

6. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously:

  • All network communications use HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Sensitive data (such as API configuration) is stored in the iOS Keychain
  • No user accounts or passwords are required
  • Server-side processing uses secure Firebase Functions with no persistent data storage
  • The App does not track you across other apps or websites

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have control over your data:

  • Calendar Access: You can revoke calendar permission at any time via iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Calendars.
  • Analytics: You can limit analytics collection via iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.
  • Delete All Local Data: Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.
  • Subscription Management: Manage or cancel subscriptions via your Apple ID settings.
  • Data Deletion Request: Contact us at wisecal.help@outlook.com to request deletion of any data associated with your device.

8. Children's Privacy

WiseCal is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete such data.

9. International Data Transfers

Extracted text may be processed by servers located outside your country of residence (Google Cloud infrastructure). By using the App, you consent to the transfer of this non-personal data to these servers. No personally identifiable information is transferred.

10. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to request disclosure of the categories of personal information we collect and the purposes for which it is used. As we do not collect personally identifiable information, there is no personal data to disclose, delete, or opt out of selling (we do not sell any user data).

Since WiseCal does not collect personally identifiable information, the data we process (anonymous analytics) falls outside the scope of personal data under GDPR. However, you may contact us to exercise any rights you believe apply.
